# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and behavior without personal
attacks, harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or disclosure of private
information.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository maintainer or GitHub's reporting
tools. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible.
