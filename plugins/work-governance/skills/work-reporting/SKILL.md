# Work Reporting

Reporting supports decisions and continuity. Keep it semantic and proportional;
never force a canned sentence or a field-by-field ritual that obscures the real
state.

## While Work Continues

Send concise updates often enough that the user can understand momentum without
reconstructing tool output. Prefer:

- the meaningful result or current position;
- what is happening now when it matters;
- the next action;
- a decision, risk, or surprise that needs attention.

Do not emit a complete closeout after each mechanical slice. Non-blocking
improvements and lessons may wait for the next useful checkpoint or final
report.

## Completion Report

At task completion, give a complete account in natural language. It must make
the following discoverable, using headings or prose suited to the amount of
work:

- the achieved result and how it serves the user's goal;
- what was **added**, **modified**, **deleted**, and **operated**;
- validation evidence, its boundary, and anything important not covered;
- requested work completed, deliberately not done, and still remaining;
- risks, cleanup, surprising findings, and items worth the user's attention;
- the next useful action, including any separate authority it would require.

Do not claim that nothing remains when a known obligation is open. Do not add a
formulaic “no next step” statement merely to fill a field. Omit irrelevant
detail, but never hide a meaningful deletion, external operation, unverified
claim, or unfinished requirement.

## Handoff Or Phase Close

For a real handoff, add the context another agent or future session needs:

- current goal and authoritative constraints;
- decisions and their evidence;
- relevant artifacts and exact state;
- failed attempts or invalidated routes worth avoiding;
- open questions, risks, next action, and stop conditions.

Use a persistence capability for durable handoff mechanics when available; this
Skill decides what the handoff must mean, not how a particular tool stores it.
