# Resource Observation

Assess and record consumption while advancing the authorized capability and its
acceptance. Resource observation supports judgment; it does not create a budget,
permission gate, or second execution-state system.

## Keep Capability Development Moving

Implement and verify the agreed behavior first. Do not invent Token, money,
request-count, elapsed-time, memory, or result-size ceilings to conserve
resources. Do not silently truncate evidence, weaken acceptance, choose a less
capable implementation, or pause authorized work because usage seems high or
an estimate is incomplete. A research sample's limit or an old task's allowance
is not the current development contract.

Distinguish resource protection from the feature's actual semantics. A requested
top-K result, pagination, or a demonstrated transport or context limit can be
part of a functional contract. A savings-driven cap cannot be introduced as an
unexamined implementation default. Preserve evidence completeness through
retrievable pages or batches when a real interface limit requires them; record
the constraint and any omission that affects the claim.

Routine reversible choices that preserve the outcome and acceptance remain
agent-owned. Fix redundant reads or a safe batch size when the evidence supports
it. Broader caching, compression, model substitution, throttling, quotas, or
budget enforcement belongs to a separately scoped enhancement after capability
acceptance, unless it is needed now to correct an observed correctness or
feasibility failure.

## Identify The Source Of A Constraint

- **Observation or estimate:** describe usage, trend, uncertainty, and possible
  later improvements; continue the authorized work. Correcting an estimate or
  exceeding an agent-proposed target does not reopen user confirmation.
- **Explicit binding limit:** honor an applicable user-approved spending cap,
  accepted operational contract, or enforced platform quota. Retain its source,
  scope, period, and unit; stop only the affected action before a demonstrated
  breach. Do not relax it or transfer another task's unused allowance.
- **Actual technical failure:** an out-of-memory error, rejected request, full
  disk, context overflow, or observed runaway retry requires a bounded remedy
  and validation. Continue independent work; recover inside existing authority
  instead of treating a hypothetical resource risk as a blocker.
- **New commitment or authority:** acquiring a paid service, increasing an
  authorized allowance, or changing the target, data exposure, environment, or
  other guarded action follows its existing confirmation boundary. Capability
  first does not authorize new spending commitments or unsafe effects.

If a binding limit's remaining allowance cannot be established well enough for
the next action, contain that action and resolve the missing fact or user
choice. Missing billing telemetry alone does not block ordinary work when no
binding limit applies. Name the real source and affected action for any stop;
never present an invented savings target as user or platform policy.

## Assess With Available Evidence

Use existing counters, receipts, logs, or inexpensive local observations when
they answer the current question. Do not add per-action instrumentation,
repeated polling, a complete historical audit, or a universal numerical budget
as the entry price for development.

Keep the scope and measurement method visible:

- distinguish returned-context Tokens, model-request usage, whole-task usage,
  and account-level limits; deduplicate request records and do not sum repeated
  cumulative snapshots;
- cached input is part of input and reasoning output is part of output when the
  source defines them that way; do not count those subsets twice;
- aggregate only aligned response identities, measurement windows, units, and
  counter definitions. If that alignment is unknown, report the source's
  available totals as partial evidence rather than a whole-task total;
- distinguish actual charges from estimates; a monetary estimate needs a
  applicable rate source, pricing date, currency, and usage basis. Unknown
  pricing or missing telemetry remains unknown, not zero or a reason to invent
  a cap; and
- attribute elapsed time, requests, memory, storage, or concurrency to the
  observed process, task, sample, or time window. One counter does not establish
  total cost, avoidable waste, or savings.

## Record The Smallest Useful Assessment

At an existing meaningful checkpoint, preserve only the resource evidence that
helps continuation or a later decision: task or stage and window, source and
method, measured versus estimated values with units, relevant unknowns, an
applicable limit and its authority if one exists, effect on quality or progress,
and a follow-up candidate with its consumer or re-entry condition.

Use the selected durable provider's own Skill for storage. Coalesce mechanical
observations into that checkpoint; do not duplicate its state in a plugin ledger,
record every model call, or create a Plan solely for accounting. Honor a required
provider policy without making successful telemetry collection a new execution
gate. Report an unavailable persistence route truthfully and continue unrelated
safe work. Retain one bounded pending semantic packet in active context and
carry it into an actual handoff when needed. The selected provider owns recovery,
currentness, delivery, and exact readback; do not create a second durable queue
or cross its control-plane authority to clear the packet.

## Defer Protection And Savings As An Enhancement

After capability acceptance, use recorded evidence to decide whether protection
or savings warrants work. Preserve a focused candidate: affected capability,
observed consumption or failure, proposed mechanism, quality and completeness
that must be retained, comparable baseline, and separate activation authority
where needed. Recording a candidate does not implement or activate it.

Automatic usage collection or cost attribution is observation. Initiating a
charge or purchasing a service is a financial action with its own authority.

Judge improvements against the same accepted capability and representative
inputs. Fewer calls or smaller returned context alone does not prove Token,
monetary, latency, or memory savings, and savings with lost evidence or weaker
acceptance is not equivalent delivery.
